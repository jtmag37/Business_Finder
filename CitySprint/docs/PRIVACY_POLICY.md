# City Sprint — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]. Replace bracketed fields before publishing._

City Sprint is developed by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we").

**We do not collect any personal data.** City Sprint has no accounts, no
analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and we run no servers.

## Data stored by the app

- **Game progress and settings** are stored on your device.
- **iCloud sync (optional):** if you are signed in to iCloud and leave
  "Sync progress with iCloud" on, a copy of your game progress is stored in your
  **private** iCloud database. Only you can access it. We cannot. You can turn
  this off in Settings, and "Reset progress" deletes it.

## Apple services

- **Game Center (optional):** if you sign in, scores and achievements you earn
  are sent to Apple's Game Center and may be visible to other players under your
  Game Center nickname. Apple's privacy policy applies.
- **In-app purchases** are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment
  information.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
